# Google Play draft listing

## App name
Receipt Wrangler

## Short description
Offline receipt capture, OCR review, duplicate cleanup, and packet export.

## Full description
Receipt Wrangler is a private, local-first Android utility for receipt capture and evidence management.

Capture receipts, import PDFs or screenshots, clean up difficult scans, review OCR results, merge duplicates, and export professional packets for reimbursement, tax prep, freelancer billback, gig-profit tracking, or personal records.

Everything is designed to stay on your device unless you explicitly export.

### Workflow lanes
- business expense reports
- freelancer client billback
- tax deduction cleanup
- personal household tracking
- gig economy profit wrangler
- trucker route receipts
- shoebox tax triage
- group travel split packets

### Private by default
- no account
- no cloud sync
- no analytics
- no internet permission
- on-device OCR only

### What you can do
- capture receipts with the camera
- import images and PDFs
- run on-device OCR with confidence cues
- emergency OCR fallback for rough handwritten or low-legibility captures
- clean up scans with crop, rotation, grayscale, and contrast
- choose theme mode (system, light, or dark)
- organize receipts by project stack (multiple independent stacks)
- guided first-run overlay with skip and workflow setup
- review low-confidence and duplicate queues
- batch select, categorize, archive, or merge receipts
- export CSV, backup JSON, markdown packet, full PDF packet, or gig sales PDF
- attach receipt evidence images in PDF packet exports (image and PDF sources)

## Privacy summary
Receipt Wrangler does not require an account and does not upload your receipts.
