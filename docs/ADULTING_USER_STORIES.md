# Adulting-friendly user stories

## 1. New employee filing expenses
As a newly employed adult traveling for work,
I want to snap receipts as I get them,
so I do not reconstruct a reimbursement packet from memory later.

## 2. Freelancer billing a client
As a freelancer who buys supplies for a client project,
I want to tag purchases with a report/client name and mark them billable,
so invoice backup is already assembled.

## 3. Young adult doing taxes for the first time
As someone doing taxes without loving paperwork,
I want to keep likely deduction receipts in one lane,
so tax season becomes a review pass, not a scavenger hunt.

## 4. Personal finance cleanup
As a person trying to get a grip on spending,
I want a private local ledger of receipts,
so I can review what actually happened without linking bank accounts.

## 5. Bad receipt salvage
As a user with a glare-filled or crooked photo,
I want lightweight scan cleanup controls,
so the OCR has a better shot before I type fields manually.

## 6. Duplicate merge
As a user who imported the same receipt twice,
I want to merge duplicates into one clean record,
so my totals and packet exports stay trustworthy.

## 7. PCS and duty-travel reimbursement (military)
As a newly enlisted service member or junior officer handling PCS and duty travel,
I want to capture lodging, meal, fuel, and transit receipts immediately,
so I can submit reimbursement packets without losing paperwork during moves.

## 8. Teacher / treasurer field-trip expense reporting
As a teacher or activity treasurer managing a class trip,
I want to track group expenses with clear notes, categories, and packet export,
so school finance review is straightforward and audit-friendly.

## 9. Farmers market booth / food trailer profit workflow
As a booth vendor or trailer operator,
I want an offline lane that can hold both sales receipts and expense receipts,
so I can reconcile gross sales, costs, and net profit without internet or cloud tools.

### Notes for story 9
- Use the **Gig economy profit wrangler** lane.
- Tag entries as `sale` or `expense` in notes/tags for monthly packet review.
- Keep all capture/review/export flows local-only.
- Export both full packet PDF and sales-only PDF for settlement/audit support.

## 10. Long-haul trucking compliance and reimbursement
As an owner-operator or company driver,
I want to capture fuel, lodging, toll, and repair receipts by route,
so monthly closeout and reimbursement are straightforward.

### Notes for story 10
- Use the **I'm a trucker** lane.
- Keep route/week tags consistent before packet export.

## 11. Messy shoebox triage during tax season
As someone with a backlog of mixed receipts,
I want a triage lane that prioritizes date/amount cleanup first,
so I can convert a messy pile into a clean filing packet.

### Notes for story 11
- Use the **Tax shoebox triage** lane.
- Move items Inbox → Review → Ready in staged batches.

## 12. Group travel split-cost reconciliation
As the person organizing a large shared trip,
I want to track paid receipts and split responsibilities,
so I can export one clear packet that explains shared costs.

### Notes for story 12
- Use the **Group travel split** lane.
- Use tags for payer/member and include notes before export.
